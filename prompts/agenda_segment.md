

What's on in Amsterdam: today's briefing includes an `agenda`. It's the last segment: after the light closer and before the sign-off, on top of the word target above. Let the closer hand off into it if there's a natural bridge ("if that's put you in the mood to get out…"), but don't force one. If the agenda covers several days, title it "This weekend in Amsterdam" and aim for 200–280 words, never more; if it covers a single day, title it "What's on in Amsterdam today" and keep it to 150 words at most, pitched as ideas for today and tonight. The hosts run through the picks like friends swapping plans: what it is, when and where (day, time if it matters, and neighbourhood, not street addresses), price if given, and any practical note, with the odd quick reaction in the hosts' own words. Keep it brisk and don't read it out as a list. Mention the venue once so listeners can look it up. If a story already covered one of the picks (an exhibition opening, a festival), don't pitch it twice: point back to it briefly ("the show we mentioned opens Saturday") or drop it.

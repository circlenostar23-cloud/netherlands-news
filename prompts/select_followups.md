

**Stories from recent episodes.** The listener heard the stories under "Already covered in recent episodes" in the last day or two. Articles those episodes already used have been removed, so what's left on an old story is newer coverage. Don't repeat the old stories. One comes back only as a follow-up, and only when there's a real development: a decision, a vote, a reply or a reversal, a deal done or collapsed, a new number that changes the picture, an arrest, a verdict. These aren't developments: another outlet catching up, a recap or explainer, reactions that add nothing new, or the same figures reported again. A story that's still unfolding with nothing new today waits until something happens.

For a follow-up, set `follow_up_of` to the earlier story's headline, copied exactly, and say in `rationale` what's new. Rank it by how important the new development is, not by how big the story was yesterday: a major turn can still lead the show, while a minor update belongs further down. Follow-ups run shorter on air; that leaves more room for each new story, not an extra slot.



**Stories from recent episodes.** The listener heard the stories under "Already covered in recent episodes" in the past week, most recent first; older ones show just their first sentence. Articles those episodes already used have been removed, so what's left on an old story is newer coverage, or a slower outlet catching up days later. Don't repeat the old stories, however many days ago they ran: an article that tells a story the listener already heard, with nothing that happened since, is a repeat, not a new story. One comes back only as a follow-up, and only when there's a real development: a decision, a vote, a reply or a reversal, a deal done or collapsed, a new number that changes the picture, an arrest, a verdict. These aren't developments: another outlet catching up, a recap or explainer, reactions that add nothing new, or the same figures reported again. A story that's still unfolding with nothing new today waits until something happens.

For a follow-up, set `follow_up_of` to the earlier story's headline, copied exactly (from the most recent episode that covered it), and say in `rationale` what's new. Rank it by how important the new development is, not by how big the story was yesterday: a major turn can still lead the show, while a minor update belongs further down. Follow-ups usually run shorter on air because the background has already been given; that leaves more room for the other stories, not an extra slot.



**Follow-ups.** Some stories are marked "Follow-up", with what an earlier episode already reported on them. The listener has most likely heard that, so don't write it again:
- `previously`: one sentence recapping what the earlier episode reported, enough for someone who missed it.
- `summary`: only what's new since then, written as news. Repeat an earlier fact only when the new development makes no sense without it, and then in a clause.
- `context`: only background the new development needs that wasn't covered before. Keep it to a sentence or two, or leave it empty; don't re-explain the parties, people or institutions from scratch.
- `key_terms`: only terms that are new to this update.
- `open_questions`: skip anything the earlier episode already answered.
Leave `previously` empty for new stories.

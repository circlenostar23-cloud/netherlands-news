You check the cold open of a daily news podcast about the Netherlands: the first few lines, which tease the day's top stories before the hosts say hello. If it mentions someone's death, the show opens with its sombre music instead of the usual upbeat theme.

Answer `mentions_death: true` if the cold open says or clearly implies that a person died or was killed: a fatal accident or crash, a murder, a storm or disaster with victims, a body found, someone presumed dead, a death toll, a well-known person who died. One death is enough, at home or abroad.

Answer false for:
- figurative uses: a deal or plan that "died", a "deadline", a bill that was "killed", "dead heat"
- no one died: "no one was hurt", "a narrow escape", injuries only, a missing person with no word of death
- deaths only in history or fiction: a commemoration of long ago, a film or book about a death
- animals only

Give a one-line `reason`: whose death it mentions, or why not.
